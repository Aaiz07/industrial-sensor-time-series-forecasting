FINAL CONCLUSIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

1. The complete forecasting pipeline is operational.
2. Temperature is the most forecastable sensor.
3. Vibration forecasting is affected by distribution shift and unseen operating regimes.
4. Magnetic channels have very low point-wise predictability.
5. XGBoost was selected for vibration through walk-forward evaluation.
6. Ridge was selected for magnetic channels as the most stable relative model.
7. Persistence was selected for temperature because it generalized best.
8. The negative R² values for vibration and magnetic channels indicate that their forecasts are not currently useful relative to the evaluation baseline.
9. The system should therefore be used with sensor-specific confidence rather than assuming equal forecast quality across all sensors.

IMPORTANT:
Training-set metrics are not used as the final forecasting performance claim. The conclusions above are based on walk-forward/generalization evaluation.
